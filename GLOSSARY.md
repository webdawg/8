# Glossary — Dark Matter Manifolds

Terms defined over the course of building `dark-matter-manifolds.html`, sourced from the original prompts in `llm-original-prompt/`.

## Dark Matter
The thin layer spread everywhere that separates the fields of the universe. Described as "an infinite field transformer with tensor flows to the infinite power." Visualized as the drip layer falling through the gap between the two side manifolds.

## The Fields (left / right manifolds)
Two energy-based manifolds, one on each side, that can never touch because they exist in different dimensions of space. Each is a calculus manifold — a flat plane shape that crosses multiple dimensions, represented in 3D (x, y, z). Made of complex energy: lines and things that continuously change vectors as they move forward.

## Ribbon Leg Field
The white tube object living inside the left manifold. A pair of tube-shaped ribbons sharing a hip, splayed apart so they cross — visually "an intersecting set of two ribbons," with one leg's foot resting higher than the other's. A single wall line cuts through the middle of their hip joint as a barrier between them.
